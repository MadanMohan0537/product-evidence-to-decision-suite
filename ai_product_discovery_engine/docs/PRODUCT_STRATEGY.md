# Product Strategy

## Positioning

An evidence system for discovery, not an AI roadmap oracle. The wedge is local-first synthesis of exported feedback, with traceability and transparent ranking as the differentiator.

## Sequence

1. Prove reliable CSV/JSON synthesis and evidence retrieval.
2. Measure opportunity acceptance and false-positive themes with real PMs.
3. Add one connector end to end, including cursors, retries, and deletion handling.
4. Add semantic embeddings only when they improve a labeled retrieval benchmark.
5. Introduce continuous monitoring after stable topic identity and tenant isolation exist.

## Moat hypothesis

The durable value is the customer's evidence graph, correction history, segment context, and evaluation loop—not access to a general-purpose model.

## Risks

Automation bias, weak source representation, duplicated customers, sensitive feedback, unsupported causal claims, semantic-cluster instability, and persuasive summaries that exceed their evidence.
