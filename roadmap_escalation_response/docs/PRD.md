# Product requirements

User: product or operations lead following up on overdue prevention-control handoffs.

Job: distinguish escalation preparation from recorded sending and acknowledgment, preserving the originating evidence lineage.

Acceptance criteria:

1. Only recomputed eligible Project 67 drafts enter the connected assessment.
2. Receipts bind uniquely to control, contact, channel, and runbook.
3. UTC chronology and a 1–720 hour integer policy are enforced.
4. Missing routes and send records remain visible; exact deadlines and late replies are deterministic.
5. The frontend renders text safely and uses existing light/dark styles and authenticated API access.
6. No automatic sending, remediation, ownership transfer, or persistence is implied.

Non-goals: email delivery, ticket connectors, response identity attestation, real-time paging, legal audit certification, or a remediation verdict.
