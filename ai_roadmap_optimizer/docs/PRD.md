# Product requirements

## User and job

Product managers and engineering leaders need to convert a scored backlog into a roadmap that respects delivery reality. They must compare strategic choices without hiding capacity, dependencies or input assumptions.

## MVP acceptance criteria

- Reject malformed backlogs, duplicate IDs, missing teams and dependency cycles.
- Never schedule a dependent item before its prerequisites finish.
- Never exceed declared team capacity or a selected feature's deadline.
- Explain what was omitted without inventing a causal narrative.
- Produce the same output for identical input.
- Compare balanced, growth, revenue, retention and low-risk objectives.
- Keep all estimates user supplied and all scoring weights visible in code.

## Not in MVP

No Jira connector, saved workspace, user authentication, live staffing calendar, automatic impact prediction, Monte Carlo simulation, causal inference or automatic roadmap approval.
