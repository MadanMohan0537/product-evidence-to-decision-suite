# Product and model metrics

- Constraint violation rate: must remain zero on accepted plans.
- Feasible-plan rate: percentage of valid inputs with at least one roadmap.
- Solve latency by backlog size and optional-feature count.
- Scenario stability: overlap and rank movement between objectives.
- Override rate: percentage of recommendations changed by the accountable team.
- Estimate calibration: forecast effort and value versus observed outcomes.
- Roadmap completion rate and dependency-blocked time.

Do not optimize adoption alone: a frequently accepted but infeasible roadmap is a product failure.
